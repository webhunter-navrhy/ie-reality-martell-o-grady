Martell O'Grady Estate Agents are delighted to present St. Theresa's, an exceptional detached family home occupying a generous, private site on Pearse Road. Beautifully maintained and extensively upgraded over the years, this impressive residence seamlessly combines contemporary finishes with generous living accommodation, offering a rare opportunity to acquire a substantial family home within walking distance of Sligo Town Centre.

A striking entrance with feature stained-glass panels leads into a bright and welcoming hallway, setting the tone for the quality found throughout the home. Internally, the accommodation has been thoughtfully decorated to retain the warmth and character expected of a family home.

The elegant front sitting room provides a relaxing retreat, while the spacious kitchen is finished with sleek modern cabinetry, quality integrated appliances and generous workspace. A magnificent timber-vaulted sunroom enjoys an abundance of natural light and overlooks the private rear garden, creating an ideal space for family gatherings.

Accommodation is exceptionally versatile, with well-proportioned bedrooms on the ground floor complemented by a converted attic offering valuable additional accommodation, ideal as a home office or hobby space (subject to any necessary planning or building regulation requirements). A second living area with its own kitchen further enhances the flexibility of the home, making it suitable for those seeking additional space.

The property also benefits from a three-phase electricity supply, being one of only four properties on Pearse Road with this feature, providing enhanced electrical capacity suitable for modern living, workshops, home businesses or future upgrades.

The private rear garden provides an excellent outdoor space, featuring a timber deck, complemented by mature planting, extensive paved areas, and a detached garden shed.

To the front and side, the property benefits from a private driveway providing ample parking.

LOCATION

Pearse Road remains one of Sligo's most established residential locations, offering exceptional convenience. Residents enjoy easy access to Sligo Town Centre, shops, cafés, restaurants, healthcare facilities and excellent transport links, while the area's renowned beaches, mountain walks and outdoor amenities are all within a short drive.

Families will particularly appreciate the excellent selection of nearby schools, including Our Lady of Mercy National School, St. John's National School, Mercy College, Summerhill College, Sligo Grammar School and Ursuline College, all within easy reach.
